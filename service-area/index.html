How coverage works

Five towns, one dispatch desk

Jenks Pro Plumbing works from 300 Riverwalk Terrace in Jenks, Oklahoma, in Tulsa County. From that address dispatch sends licensed and insured plumbers to Bixby, Glenpool, Sapulpa and Kiefer. The emergency line is open 24/7 for all five towns, and the number is the same no matter where you live.

You do not need to find a separate office for your town. Call (855) 575-2890, say your street and ZIP, and dispatch will tell you what same-day time is open. Visits are quoted with a clear price before work starts, wherever the house is.

Find your place

Which town and ZIP code are you in?

Mailing addresses can fool you, because the Tulsa ZIPs sit close to several of these towns. Check your water bill, your property tax notice or the city name on your utility account to see which town you are in.

If you are unsure, give dispatch the street address and the nearest crossroads. That is enough to know if the address is inside the five-town area and which city rules apply to the work.

ZIP by ZIP

One ZIP at a time, tied to what you will see

Each town has a single main ZIP code. Here is what matters in each, and where to read more.

Jenks, 74037

Census ACS data for 2020 to 2024 shows a median Jenks build year of 2006. Calls here are mostly water heaters, fixtures and drains rather than old pipe, and the [[|Jenks homepage]] covers what to do in an emergency.

Bixby, 74008

The Arkansas River splits Bixby, and the US-64 Memorial Drive bridge is the only crossing in the city, according to the city's housing study. The same study notes low water pressure in a few outlying subdivisions. Details are on the Bixby emergency plumber page.

Glenpool, 74033

Glenpool grew after the 1905 Glenn Pool oil strike, and the Census ACS shows a median build year of 1995 with a good share of 1970s and 1980s homes. That older block is where supply lines and water heaters age out. See the Glenpool plumber page.

Sapulpa, 74066

Sapulpa has the oldest housing of the five, with 55 percent of homes built before 1980 per Census ACS. Older supply lines and drains are more likely here, so ask the plumber to check them. The Sapulpa page explains more, and the 74067 ZIP is a PO Box point inside 74066.

Kiefer, 74041

Kiefer is a small town on SH-67 and Alternate US-75 whose water comes through Rural Water District #2. Because it is a small system, low pressure on the public side is a call to the district first. The Kiefer page covers the rest.

Your first steps while you wait

Do these before the plumber arrives, whichever town you are in.

  • ✓Shut the main. Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops, then open the lowest faucet to drain the line.
  • ✓Cut power near water. Switch off breakers for wet rooms and the water heater if water is near them.
  • ✓Stop using drains. If sewage is coming up, do not flush or run sinks until the line is cleared.
  • ✓Gas smell? Go outside. Call the gas utility or 911 from outside before you call a plumber.
