About these guides

Read first, then decide whether to call

The articles below explain plumbing problems the way a plumber would explain them at your kitchen counter. Each one covers likely causes, what a homeowner can reasonably try, and the point where a licensed plumber should take over. They are written for homes in Jenks and the surrounding towns, where most houses are newer and the most common calls involve water heaters, fixtures and drains.

The guides are information only. They cannot see your pipes, so they cannot replace a visit. If water is spreading, a drain is backing up into the house, or you smell gas, do not wait to finish reading. Call the 24/7 line at (855) 575-2890, or for gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Local facts in the articles come from public sources, and each one names the type of source, such as Census data or a City of Jenks page. Where we do not have a verified local fact, the guide says to test your tap or ask the city instead of guessing.

How we write

What you will and will not find here

You will find real parts and methods: shutoff valves, P-traps, anode rods, cleanouts, supply lines and drain augers. You will find honest limits on DIY work, including when a fix is fine to try and when it risks a bigger repair.

You will not find exact prices, because price depends on access, parts and the time of the call. We give a clear price before work starts. You also will not find medical advice about water quality. Ask your physician about health questions, and see the about page for what we do and do not do.

If a guide describes a problem you have and you want a plumber, the services list shows the matching pages. You can also look up any plumber's licence through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board before you hire.
