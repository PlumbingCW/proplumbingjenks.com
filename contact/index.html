Safety first

While you wait for the plumber

For an active leak, close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house. For a water heater leak, turn off its water supply and, if you can reach it safely, the power or gas control.

For sewage backing up, stop using drains and toilets. Move belongings out of the wet area and keep children and pets away from it.
